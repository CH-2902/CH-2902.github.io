Final-year project at NTU under **Prof. Seah Hock Soon**, exploring novel
methods to improve training efficiency for reinforcement-learning agents in
a physically realistic table-tennis environment.

**Highlights**

- Implemented **Soft Actor-Critic (SAC)** agents in a custom Unity ML-Agents
  table-tennis environment.
- Designed a **custom curriculum-learning strategy** that cut training time
  by **50%** while maintaining a baseline of 20-shot rallies.
- Iteratively tuned dynamic hyperparameters to push agents to consistently
  perform **advanced backhand top-spin rallies** — comparable to professional
  human play.
- Planned and executed the full 12-month roadmap, syncing milestones and
  evaluation benchmarks with my supervisor and project manager.

**Result:** Awarded grade **A-**.

**Stack:** Python · PyTorch · Unity ML-Agents · C#.
