A data-visualisation project exploring the global distribution of billionaire
wealth across countries, industries, and demographics.

**What I did**

- Cleaned and pre-processed the raw Kaggle dataset — handled missing values,
  removed duplicates, standardised column conventions.
- Conducted **exploratory data analysis** with Pandas, Seaborn, and Matplotlib
  to surface trends in wealth concentration, sector dominance, and self-made
  vs. inherited splits.
- Built an **interactive Plotly Dash dashboard** with linked filters across
  country, industry, and wealth source so reviewers could drill into any
  slice on demand.

**Stack:** Python · Pandas · Seaborn · Matplotlib · Plotly · Dash.
